The Method

Antesigna turns the visible positions of 100 screened Hyperliquid traders into a single aggregate market read. It measures positioning; it does not predict returns or expose individual traders.

1. The Hundred, not a leaderboard

The tracked set is selected by a proprietary multi-signal process that evaluates performance context and observed trading behavior across multiple time horizons. Its job is to distinguish accounts expressing durable directional information from activity that could create a misleading picture of conviction.

No single rank, position, or recent win is enough. Selection inputs, transformations, weights, thresholds, internal scores, and wallet identities remain private. We publish what the product measures, how to interpret it, and its limitations—not a recipe for reproducing the tracker.

2. Tracker integrity

The Hundred are reconsidered weekly and must pass automated checks for data sufficiency, set stability, structural integrity, and aggregate sanity. If a proposed refresh fails those controls, the previous accepted set remains live.

Accepted changes are deployed atomically. Movement comparisons are clamped at a cohort change, so selection turnover is not presented as trader activity.

3. Metric definitions

Gross and net exposure

Gross exposure is the sum of absolute long and short notionals. Net exposure is long notional minus short notional.

Tilt and conviction

Asset tilt is asset net exposure divided by asset gross exposure: +1 is entirely long, −1 entirely short, and values near zero are balanced. Conviction is a normalized aggregate measure of directional agreement among active positions.

Signum

Signum is a normalized summary of aggregate direction and exposure. Positive readings indicate net-long positioning and negative readings net-short positioning. Magnitude reflects positioning intensity—not a probability, price target, or forecast. Exact transformations and weighting are proprietary.

4. Public privacy boundary

The dashboard publishes market-level aggregates only when at least three tracked traders hold that market. Thinner markets are combined into a coarsely rounded, symbol-free aggregate. Per-side totals and counts are not published. It does not publish wallet addresses or exact per-wallet position/equity pairs; position and equity distributions use separate fixed, broad buckets.

5. Cadence and alerts

The public dashboard refreshes hourly. The free Telegram feed publishes scheduled reads. The Watch evaluates the complete tracked market universe after each successful refresh and contacts subscribers only when an aggregate regime, net exposure, tilt, or conviction change clears its materiality threshold.

The Ledger is the permanent public record of scheduled aggregate snapshots. Watch notifications are a private subscriber service and are not republished into the public Ledger.

6. Known limitations

  • Hyperliquid-visible positions may be hedged on another venue or in spot.
  • Historical success does not guarantee useful or persistent future signal.
  • On-chain data, third-party APIs, and calculations can be delayed, incomplete, or erroneous.
  • A screened trader or the aggregate read can be early, wrong, squeezed, or liquidated.
  • The methodology and results have not been independently audited.
